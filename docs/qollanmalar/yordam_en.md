# ILBI: getting started and support

Updated: **2026-10-14**.

## Want to connect your shop?

You can contact support without an account. Open **Help center** on the sign-in page. Phone: **+998 77 021 21 49**. Telegram operator: **@abdulxadiy_0**.

## Contact an operator

1. Select English and choose **New conversation**.
2. Enter your name, a subject and your question. Read and accept the notice about storing your messages and images and making them available to support operators, then submit.
3. The reply appears in this chat. Guest access uses a secret key saved in your current browser. Clearing browser data removes that key; the conversation does not automatically appear on another device.
4. If helpful, attach a PNG, JPEG or WebP image up to 2 MB. Capture only the area relevant to your question.

**Waiting for operator** means your request is queued. **In progress** means an operator accepted it. **Closed** means the conversation ended. Choose **Reopen** if you still need help.

Working hours and a guaranteed response time have not been published. Your message is saved for an operator to reply. The AI assistant is planned for a later stage; automated AI replies are not enabled.

## Already have an account?

Sign in with your username, phone or email and password, then enter the Telegram code if requested. The sign-in bot is **@ilbi_bot**; the human operator's Telegram account is **@abdulxadiy_0**.

After sign-in, role guides explain the workflows available to you. Owners manage their shops; employees use the permissions granted by their owner. Detailed role guides are currently in Uzbek. The support interface is available in English, Russian and Uzbek.

Retail POS currently records transactions in UZS only. Production sales are blocked until the fiscal integration is ready. Sales screenshots in the guides use a synthetic demo environment.

## Your support data

Platform operators can view your messages and attachments. Telegram notifications contain a link to the operator panel, without conversation text or images. Do not send passwords, OTP codes, bank card details or unnecessary customer data. Contact the operator above with questions about your personal data. The full privacy policy is available in Uzbek in the document selector.
